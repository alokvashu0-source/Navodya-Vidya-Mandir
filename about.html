About Our Institution

Established in 2011 — Proudly Serving Chandausi

Our Rich History

In 2011, Navodaya Vidya Mandir School opened its doors with a simple but profound dream: to offer localized, highly accessible, and rigorous educational options to families in Chandausi, Uttar Pradesh. Starting with a modest infrastructure, the institution set out to cater specifically to students from primary stages up to class 8th.

Under the UP Board system, we introduced custom pathways allowing children to study comfortably in Hindi Medium or English Medium based on their comfort and potential. Over the years, our campus at Pragati Vihar, Moradabad Road has evolved into a bustling center of multi-dimensional development.

Our Vision

"To build a legacy of conscious learning, where quality bilingual education transforms young boys and girls into responsible citizens equipped with the modern intellect, and strong moral characters."

Our Mission

"To create a supportive, secure learning space that encourages children to grow continuously. We strive to deliver balanced instruction up to 8th standard, honoring individual differences and unlocking creativity."
