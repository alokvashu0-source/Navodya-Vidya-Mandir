Admission Guidelines

Navodaya Vidya Mandir welcomes admission requests for classes Playgroup/Nursery up to Class 8th. We look forward to evaluating candidates based on general capability interviews and interactions.

Documents required during final physical verification:

  • Valid birth certificate of the student.
  • Transfer Certificate (TC) from the previous recognized school (if applicable).
  • Aadhaar Card of the Student & Parents.
  • Passport size photos of the student (4 copies).

Please complete this online registration form. Our office representative will reach out to you within 2 working days via phone to schedule an interactive baseline session.
